A little about me

Helping students turn participation into growth.

My work sits at the intersection of student engagement, leadership, and community-building.

As an Officer in Student Club and Activity at BINUS Malang, I support student life beyond the classroom. I’m interested in how organizations can become places where people feel they belong, take initiative, work together, and learn by doing.

Alongside my campus role, my interests include team resilience, leadership, and team creativity—topics I have explored through academic research.
